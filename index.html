01 / Movement

Your arms are the engine.

Forget traditional movement. Grab, push, swing and launch yourself through the world.

Lethal Ape turns physical movement into the heart of the experience. Crawl through tight spaces, scramble over obstacles, reach for ledges and throw yourself around corners when something starts getting too close.

Every movement becomes part of the strategy. Knowing how to move is one thing. Knowing when to move is another.

Try the movement

02 / The Facility

Something is always moving.

Dark corridors. Strange rooms. Hidden routes. Places you probably shouldn't be.

Explore the depths of the facility with your friends, searching for routes, secrets and a way forward. The further you go, the harder it becomes to know what is waiting around the next corner.

03 / The Experience

Built around the moments you remember.

The best rounds aren't scripted. They happen because everyone makes a different decision at the worst possible time.

01 — PHYSICAL

Move for real.

Every jump, climb and desperate escape comes from your own movement. Your body becomes part of the strategy.

02 — SOCIAL

Make noise together.

Coordinate with friends, split up when necessary, regroup when things go wrong and laugh about the player who got caught first.

03 — UNPREDICTABLE

Expect the unexpected.

Different players, routes and decisions can make a familiar location feel completely different on the next run.

04 — TENSION

Listen carefully.

Sometimes the most important information is the sound coming from somewhere you can't see.

05 — REPLAYABLE

One more round.

Survive, get caught, switch modes and jump straight back in. There is always another route worth testing.

06 — CHAOTIC

Plans rarely survive.

A perfect strategy can disappear the second the lights change and someone starts screaming down the hallway.

05 / Lore

Nobody remembers who built it.

They only remember what happens after the doors close.

Beneath the surface sits a place that feels too large, too old and too active to have been abandoned. Corridors connect to rooms that should not fit together. Machines hum behind walls. Footsteps appear where nobody is standing.

The deeper you go, the less the facility feels like a location and the more it feels like something watching the people inside it.

ENTRY

The doors open.

The first step is always easy. The way back is the part nobody talks about.

DESCENT

The map stops making sense.

Routes disappear, familiar rooms return and the group starts noticing things that weren't there before.

CONTACT

You hear something nearby.

At first it sounds distant. Then it sounds closer. Then everyone starts moving at once.

ESCAPE

Find a way back.

Getting out is not about being the fastest. It's about making the right decision before the next mistake.

10 / Questions

Before you descend.

A few quick answers for new players.

What platform is Lethal Ape on?

Lethal Ape is available for Meta Quest. Check the official store listing for the latest supported headset information.

Is the game multiplayer?

Yes. Multiplayer is a major part of the experience, with gameplay built around playing alongside or against other people.

Do I need to be good at VR movement?

No. You can learn the movement as you play. Physical movement is intentionally part of the experience.

Is Lethal Ape still being updated?

Lethal Ape is an evolving experience, so features, balance, content and presentation may change during development.

Where can I find official announcements?

The official community channels and Meta Quest store listing are the best places to check for current information.
